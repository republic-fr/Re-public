# Title XI — Public finances {#finances-publiques}

## Chapter I — Budgetary principles

### Article 101 — General principles {#finances-principes-generaux #separation-fonctions-budgetaires}

In accordance with Article [4](#separation-pouvoirs), the determination of taxation and the authorisation of expenditure fall within the law adopted by the citizens' conventions, the execution of expenditure falls to the executive administration, and the oversight of the use of public funds falls to the Law Implementation Oversight Authority and the Court of Audit.

Public finances are governed by the following principles:

- annuality: the budget is voted for one year; multi-year commitment authorisations are possible for investments;

- unity: all revenue and all expenditure appear in a single document submitted to the annual ordinary convention;

- universality: all revenue covers all expenditure, without specific earmarking, except for a reasoned exception provided for by organic law;

- specificity: appropriations are specified by programme and by purpose, without redeployment outside authorisation;

- sincerity: budget forecasts are sincere, comprehensive and free of any concealment;

- balance: the Republic strives for balanced public accounts; derogations are reasoned and limited in time.

### Article 102 — Reinforced principles {#finances-principes-renforces}

This Constitution further enshrines:

- full transparency: every person has the right to an accessible, sincere, intelligible and detailed presentation of revenue, expenditure, commitments, transfers, public contracts and the results of public action;

- ecological and social sustainability: the budget includes an assessment of its ecological, social and gender impact, according to methods laid down by organic law;

- equity between generations: public debt may not exceed, save in reasoned exceptional circumstances, a level set by the agenda-setting convention.

## Chapter II — The two-stage budget cycle

### Article 103 — Multi-year guidelines {#budget-convention-programmation}

Every five years, the agenda-setting convention sets for the coming period:

- indicative multi-year envelopes for each major area of public action;

- the main tax guidelines, in particular the overall level of compulsory levies, the balance between direct and indirect taxation, progressivity, ecological and social taxation and the policy on tax loopholes;

- the balance rules for the period, in particular the maximum debt trajectory and the rules applicable in the event of a crisis;

- multi-year investment priorities;

- the principles of territorial equality, in particular the equalisation rules, grants, the own resources of local authorities and transfers of powers accompanied by the corresponding resources.

These decisions set structural guidelines, not detailed amounts. They are binding on the annual ordinary convention without taking the place of its deliberation.

### Article 104 — Annual budget and tax laws {#budget-convention-ordinaire #determination-impot}

The annual ordinary convention adopts each year:

- the State budget, in revenue and expenditure, broken down by ministry and by programme;

- the social security budget, in revenue and expenditure;

- the annual tax laws, in particular rates schedules, rates, adjustment measures and new tax measures of limited scope, in compliance with the multi-year tax guidelines;

- borrowing authorisations.

It verifies that the budget complies with the multi-year priorities set by the agenda-setting convention. It may derogate from these priorities by the qualified majority defined in Article [17](#majorite-qualifiee), by reasoned decision, in particular in the event of significant new circumstances.

Major tax reforms, in particular the creation, abolition or overhaul of a significant tax, fall within a dedicated thematic convention, opened by the Civic Agenda Authority at the reasoned request of the agenda-setting convention or of the annual ordinary convention. In the event of a dispute over the nature of a tax measure, any constitutional authority may refer the matter to the Constitutional Council, which determines the competent convention.

### Article 105 — Preparation of the draft budget {#preparation-projet-budget}

The executive administration prepares the draft budget and transmits it to the annual ordinary convention, without any power of approval.

To prevent any technical capture and to inform deliberation, the technical service of the Law Implementation Oversight Authority draws up an independent analysis report on the draft. This report identifies in particular areas of opacity, insufficiently justified expenditure and discrepancies with the multi-year guidelines. The convention deliberates in the light of both documents.

## Chapter III — Expenditure, execution and oversight

### Article 106 — Authorisation of expenditure {#autorisation-depense}

Public expenditure is authorised by the annual ordinary convention, at the level of the budget programme. The executive administration executes expenditure within these limits, and may not redeploy appropriations between programmes without new authorisation.

### Article 107 — In-year expenditure {#depenses-cours-annee}

In the event of an urgent need arising during the year, in particular in the event of a crisis falling under Title [XII](#crises), the emergency convention may authorise, by qualified majority, the strictly necessary additional expenditure. These authorisations shall be confirmed by the following annual ordinary convention.

In the event of an immediate threat to financial stability, the minister responsible for finance may, with the agreement of the member who chairs the college of ministers, grant a State guarantee within the limit of a ceiling set each year by the annual ordinary convention together with the budget. The matter is referred to the emergency convention without delay; failing confirmation within seventy-two hours, the guarantee ceases for the future. A public account of it is given to the plenary session of the Law Implementation Oversight Authority.

Outside a crisis, an additional session of the annual ordinary convention may be convened, on referral by the Civic Agenda Authority, when new expenditure of significant scope cannot wait for the following ordinary session.

### Article 108 — Oversight of budget execution {#budget-controle-execution #rapport-annuel-execution}

The technical service of the Law Implementation Oversight Authority oversees budget execution, in particular:

- the regularity of operations with regard to accounting and budgetary rules;

- the sincerity of the public accounts;

- the effectiveness of expenditure and the evaluation of public policies;

- compliance with the authorisations of the annual ordinary convention and the guidelines of the agenda-setting convention.

It draws up each year a budget execution report, presented to the plenary session and then transmitted to the following annual ordinary convention. That convention may draw the consequences, in particular through budgetary reorientations, requests for a corrective thematic convention or referral to the Court of Public Integrity and Accountability in the event of serious misconduct attributable to a public office-holder.

## Chapter IV — Deadlocks and overruns

### Article 109 — Deadlock in adopting the budget {#blocage-adoption-budget}

If the annual ordinary convention fails to adopt the budget by the required majority, the previous budget is renewed on a provisional basis, within the strict limits necessary for the continuity of public services, without new programmes or increases. This renewal may not exceed three months.

At the end of this period, the General Authority for Citizens' Conventions convenes a new session of the annual ordinary convention, composed of citizens newly drawn by lot. If this second examination fails, the matter is automatically referred to the emergency convention.

### Article 110 — Excessive deficit or debt {#deficit-dette-excessifs}

When budget execution reveals a deficit or debt exceeding the rules set by the agenda-setting convention, the Law Implementation Oversight Authority records this in its annual report. The following annual ordinary convention shall publicly give reasons for maintaining or correcting it. It may derogate from these rules under the conditions of Article [104](#budget-convention-ordinaire).

## Chapter V — Territorial finances

### Article 111 — Territorial taxation and equalisation {#architecture-fiscale-territoriale #perequation}

Within the framework set by the agenda-setting convention, territorial conventions may set the rates of the local taxes from which they benefit, within the limits provided for by national law.

Equalisation between local authorities is organised by national law, in order to guarantee effective equality between territories and solidarity between local authorities.
