# Title VI — Scientific review {#controle-scientifique}

### Article 45 — Principle and ad hoc Scientific Council {#controle-scientifique-principe #conseil-scientifique-ad-hoc}

Every text adopted by a citizens' convention is subject, before its entry into force, to a scientific review strictly limited to the cases provided for by this Constitution, within the time limits laid down in Article [39](#controle-prealable-automatique-lois).

This review is carried out by an ad hoc Scientific Council, constituted for each text according to the disciplines concerned, as soon as the convention settles its draft. It is dissolved after delivering its opinion on the adopted text.

### Article 46 — Scientific pool and appointment {#vivier-scientifique-designation}

A permanent public pool of experts is constituted according to objective, verifiable criteria laid down by the organic law. Holders of a doctorate or an equivalent university qualification in a recognised scientific discipline are automatically entered in it, as are researchers working in a public research establishment, a university or a recognised institute whose research activity is attested by publications.

Any other person meeting the entry criteria may apply for entry by demonstrating their qualifications through peer-reviewed scientific publications or verifiable contributions to the discipline. The application is examined according to objective criteria laid down by the organic law.

The pool is stratified by disciplines and sub-disciplines according to a nomenclature laid down by the organic law. A person may appear in several sub-disciplines according to their qualifications. The lists are kept up to date by universities, public research establishments, learned societies and academies for their members, under the supervision of the General Authority for Citizens' Conventions.

The organic law shall lay down the conditions of accreditation, in particular scientific quality, disciplinary and methodological diversity and the absence of conflicts of interest. The principles common to the pools, in particular appeal against a refusal of entry and review of plurality, are laid down in Article [132](#vivier-public-experts).

For each text, the members of the ad hoc Scientific Council are drawn by lot from the pool, on a stratified basis, to guarantee disciplinary, methodological and school-of-thought pluralism. The convention examining the text may challenge any appointed member for manifest conflict of interest. The Civic Agenda Authority reviews the plurality of the pool drawn upon for each Council.

### Article 47 — Safeguards of independence {#controle-scientifique-independance}

The term of office of members of the ad hoc Scientific Council is short and not immediately renewable. Their remuneration is uniform and independent of reputation. Any dissenting opinion shall be reasoned, published and added to the public file.

### Article 48 — Scope of review {#controle-scientifique-champ}

The ad hoc Scientific Council intervenes only where there is sufficient scientific consensus on the existence of a manifest scientific error causing harm:

- to people's health;

- to their physical or mental integrity;

- to their environment.

The existence of this consensus is established by a two-thirds majority of the members of the Council. It may be challenged before the Constitutional Council, under the conditions laid down by the organic law.

Before the final vote, the Council also delivers a public opinion on the foreseeable economic and social effects of the text. This opinion concerns the expected effects, not the objectives or the political choices of the text. It has no suspensive effect. A convention that departs from it shall give public reasons for its decision.

### Article 49 — Suspension {#controle-scientifique-suspension #controle-scientifique-publicite}

The ad hoc Scientific Council has exclusively a power of suspension. It does not rule on the political expediency of the text, which falls to the convention.

Any suspension shall be based on specific objections, be public and be strictly limited to the objections established. The reasoned opinion, the dissenting opinions and the file are published in full. The suspended text is re-examined under the conditions laid down in Article [23](#reexamen-cas-suspension).
