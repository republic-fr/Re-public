# Title I — Fundamental principles {#principes}

### Article 1 — Deliberative Republic and equality {#republique-egalite}

France is a deliberative democratic Republic, indivisible and secular.

It ensures the equality of all citizens before the law, without distinction of origin, social condition, sex, gender, sexual orientation, age, state of health, disability, belief or religion.

The Republic guarantees equal access for all, without distinction of sex or gender, to public, professional, social and economic responsibilities.

### Article 2 — Language, emblem, anthem, motto and principle {#langue-embleme-devise}

The language of the Republic is French.

Regional and minority languages belong to the cultural heritage of France and are protected by law. Their teaching is guaranteed, and their use is recognised in local public life, under conditions laid down by law.

The national emblem is the tricolour flag, blue, white and red.

The national anthem is ‘La Marseillaise’.

The motto of the Republic is ‘Liberty, Equality, Fraternity’.

Its principle is government of the people, by the people and for the people.

### Article 3 — Sovereignty and means of exercise {#souverainete-modes-exercice}

Sovereignty belongs to the people, formed by the civic body defined in Article [13](#eligibilite-participation).

It is normally exercised through citizens' conventions drawn by lot, representative of the social, territorial and demographic diversity of the Republic, and, in the cases provided for by this Constitution, by direct universal suffrage.

### Article 4 — Separation of powers {#separation-pouvoirs}

Six functions are exercised by separate authorities:

- making the law, including the finance law, which determines taxation and authorises expenditure;

- organising citizen deliberation;

- reviewing the conformity of the law with the Constitution and, within the limits laid down in Title [VI](#controle-scientifique), its scientific validity;

- implementing the law, which includes command of the armed forces;

- overseeing the implementation of the law and the use of public funds, with the assistance of the Court of Audit, a financial court, under the conditions provided for in Article [66](#juridiction-financiere-service-technique);

- adjudicating.

The deployment of the armed forces is decided by a citizens' convention. Only strictly defensive operations in the event of direct aggression and the urgent operations exhaustively listed in Article [61](#autorisation-operations-militaires) may be undertaken by the executive authority, under the conditions provided for in that Article.

In the event of crisis, the emergency convention may temporarily exercise several of these functions, under the conditions laid down in Title [XII](#crises), with the exception of the judicial function and constitutional review.

### Article 5 — Political parties and groups {#partis-groupements-politiques}

Political parties and groups are formed and operate freely. They contribute to the expression of pluralism. The expression of political ideas, including minority, critical or dissenting ones, is protected.

They have no role of their own in the making of the law, the appointment of public office-holders or the organisation of citizens' conventions, which are based on civic sortition.

A political party or group may not be dissolved on account of the ideas or opinions it publicly defends. Dissolution may be ordered only by the ordinary courts, by a reasoned decision following adversarial proceedings, and only for serious material acts undermining the constitutional order or for a set of final convictions revealing a systemic modus operandi.

Title [XIV](#probite-deliberative) lays down the conditions for dissolution as well as the rules on the financing of parties, the transparency of their resources and their participation in referendum campaigns.

### Article 6 — Binding force of the law {#force-obligatoire-loi}

Every public authority is bound by the letter and the purpose of the law adopted in accordance with the Constitution. No ground of urgency, expediency or convenience may justify evading it.

### Article 7 — Protection against the tyranny of the majority, resistance and disobedience {#tyrannie-majorite-resistance}

The Republic guarantees every person protection against the tyranny of the majority. No law, act of implementation, crisis measure or constitutional amendment may infringe the rights declared inviolable by this Constitution.

Every person, whether a citizen, a public servant or a member of the armed forces, has the right and the duty to refuse to assist any undertaking whose object or effect is to overthrow the constitutional order, to infringe inviolable rights or to abolish citizen oversight of the institutions.

No one may be prosecuted or punished for having refused to carry out an order manifestly contrary to the Constitution. Having carried out such an order exempts no one from responsibility.

Any act of an authority installed or maintained by force is null and void. The perpetrators and accomplices of an undertaking to overthrow the constitutional order may not benefit from any amnesty.
