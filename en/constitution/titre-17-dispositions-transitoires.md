# Title XVII — Transitional provisions {#transition}

### Article 174 — Entry into force and continuity {#transition-entree-vigueur}

This Constitution enters into force on the day the results of the referendum ratifying it are proclaimed, together with the organic laws adopted under the conditions laid down in Article [18](#application-autres-conventions).

The laws and regulations in force on that date remain applicable insofar as they are not contrary to this Constitution. Courts, administrations and local authorities continue to perform their functions until the installation of the institutions that succeed them.

Articles [7](#tyrannie-majorite-resistance) and [9](#droits-intangibles) and Title [XII](#crises) are applicable as soon as this Constitution enters into force.

### Article 175 — Preparation of the setting-up {#transition-preparation}

During the campaign for the ratification referendum, a preparatory General Authority for Citizens' Conventions prepares the setting-up of the institutions. It is formed from the services of the Economic, Social and Environmental Council (CESE), with the assistance of the public services that hold the population records. Its provisional leadership is appointed by the constituent convention, after an integrity check by the authority responsible for transparency in public life.

Subject to the adoption of this Constitution, the Preparatory Authority:

- establishes the pools provided for by this Constitution on the basis of existing professional lists;
- draws by lot the members of the emergency convention, the Civic Agenda Authority, the appointments convention, the Constitutional Council, the Court of Public Integrity and Accountability, the Standing Commission for the Oversight of Intelligence and the Armed Forces, the Independent Regulatory Authority for Democratic Life, and the non-judicial members of the High Council of the Judiciary;

- organises the election of the judicial members of the High Council of the Judiciary;
- organises the call for applications for ministerial office, the examination of applications by the technical panel and the check of the applicants' integrity.

The persons thus drawn by lot take their seats only if this Constitution is adopted.

On entry into force, the Preparatory Authority becomes the General Authority for Citizens' Conventions. Its provisional leadership remains in office until the appointment provided for in Article [26](#agcc-direction), which takes place within the year.

### Article 176 — Timetable for the setting-up {#transition-calendrier}

The emergency convention is in office as soon as this Constitution enters into force.

Within one month of entry into force, the Civic Agenda Authority, the Constitutional Council, the Court of Public Integrity and Accountability and the High Council of the Judiciary are installed, and the appointments convention appoints the ministers.

Within three months of entry into force, the plenary session of the Law Implementation Oversight Authority, the Standing Commission for the Oversight of Intelligence and the Armed Forces, the Independent Regulatory Authority for Democratic Life and the first agenda-setting convention are installed.

Where one of these steps is not completed within the prescribed time limit, the matter is automatically referred to the emergency convention.

### Article 177 — Interim period {#transition-interim}

Parliament ceases to exercise its functions on the entry into force of this Constitution.

The Government in office deals with day-to-day business until the ministers appointed take up office, under the oversight of the emergency convention. It takes no new regulatory measure, except in an emergency and subject to review by the courts.

The President of the Republic leaves office when the ministers appointed take up office. Until that date, their powers are limited to representing the Republic and ensuring the continuity of the State.

### Article 178 — Declaration of Fundamental Rights {#transition-declaration-droits}

The fundamental rights convention sits at the same time as the constituent convention. The Declaration it adopts is submitted to referendum on the same day as this Constitution, as a separate question.

If the Declaration is not adopted, the texts mentioned in the second paragraph of Article [8](#declaration-droits-fondamentaux) take its place, and a new fundamental rights convention is convened within the year.

### Article 179 — First terms {#transition-premiers-mandats}

For authorities whose members are renewed by thirds or by halves, the length of the first terms is determined by lot, so as to stagger renewals from the first period.
