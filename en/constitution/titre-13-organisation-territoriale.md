# Title XIII — Territorial organisation {#territoires}

### Article 124 — Territorial convention principle {#principe-conventionnel-territorial}

The Republic recognises the political autonomy of local authorities within their fields of competence. The deliberative bodies of local authorities are territorial citizens' conventions drawn by lot, founded on the same principles as national conventions: sortition, representativeness, protected deliberation, oversight of influence, protection of fundamental rights.

### Article 125 — Appointment and oversight of the local executive {#designation-controle-executif-local}

At each territorial level, the citizens' convention appoints the local executive team from among qualified candidates drawn in particular from the administrative services of the local authority, on the basis of skills and of an implementation project presented publicly. The local executive is strictly subordinate to the territorial convention, which periodically reviews its implementation and its budgets, and may remove it under the conditions laid down by the organic law.

### Article 126 — Municipalities {#existence-juridique-communes #mutualisation-communes}

Municipalities retain their legal existence, their local competences and their administration. Civil registration, the management of everyday services and the symbolic representation of the municipality are carried out by its administration under the supervision of the competent citizens' convention.

For municipalities whose population does not allow the constitution of a citizen pool guaranteeing sufficient representativeness, the citizens' convention of the inter-municipal body of which they are members performs the functions of the municipal convention, according to thresholds and procedures laid down by the organic law. Strictly municipal competences continue to be exercised by the municipal administration under the supervision of that convention.

### Article 127 — Division of powers {#territoires-repartition-competences #territoires-principes-directeurs}

In the fields assigned to local authorities by the Constitution or the organic law, territorial conventions have their own normative power. In other matters, they are subject to national law.

The organic law shall determine the division of powers between the national level and the territorial levels in accordance with the principles of local autonomy, subsidiarity, equality, solidarity and clarity of responsibilities. No local authority may exercise supervisory authority over another.

### Article 128 — Overseas authorities {#collectivites-outre-mer}

The Republic recognises the overseas populations within the French people, in a common ideal of liberty, equality and fraternity.

Overseas authorities are governed by special provisions taking account of their geographical, demographic, historical, cultural and linguistic specificities, in accordance with the principles of this Constitution.

The principle of the self-determination of overseas populations is guaranteed. No substantial change in the status of an authority, no modification of its own competences, and no cession, exchange or addition of territory may take place without the prior consent of the populations concerned, obtained through a dedicated territorial citizens' convention.

The provisions specific to each overseas authority, in particular the applicable legislative regimes, the powers transferred and the organisational arrangements for local institutions, are laid down by the organic law adopted after consultation of the territorial citizens' convention concerned.

The languages and cultures of the overseas territories belong to the heritage of the Republic and are protected by law.
