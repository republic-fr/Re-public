# Title IV — Deliberative Organisation Authorities {#autorites-deliberatives}

## Chapter I — The General Authority for Citizens' Conventions

### Article 25 — Nature {#agcc-nature}

The General Authority for Citizens' Conventions is an independent constitutional authority. It is responsible for the material, social and procedural organisation of the citizens' conventions provided for by this Constitution, as well as for the other constitutional stewardship tasks entrusted to it.

### Article 26 — Leadership {#agcc-direction}

The General Authority for Citizens' Conventions is headed by a Director-General.

The Director-General is appointed by the appointments convention, in accordance with the procedure laid down in Article [54](#procedure-designation-ministres), following a public call for applications and a prior integrity check by the Court of Public Integrity and Accountability.

The call for applications is in that case organised by the Law Implementation Oversight Authority, and not by the General Authority. No member of staff of the General Authority shall take part in this appointment.

The term of office is six years and is not renewable. The Director-General may be removed by the Court of Public Integrity and Accountability under the conditions laid down in Title [IX](#juridiction-probite).

### Article 27 — Tasks {#agcc-missions}

The General Authority for Citizens' Conventions:

- organises sortition, under the conditions of publicity and verification laid down in Article [13](#eligibilite-participation), and verifies representativeness;

- convenes the members of the conventions, subject to Article [14](#statut-conventionnel), and ensures their social and material protection;

- keeps up to date the public expertise pools provided for by this Constitution;

- keeps the permanent public register of influence provided for in Title [XIV](#probite-deliberative);

- guarantees the integrity of the procedure and the publicity of proceedings;

- provides neutral expertise, training and secretariat services;

- convenes the emergency convention where it fails to refer the matter to itself, under the conditions laid down in Title [XII](#crises);

- convenes a new budget session after the failure of the first, under the conditions laid down in Title [XI](#finances-publiques);

- carries out, under the supervision of the Civic Agenda Authority, the permanent administrative work of the latter, in particular the listing of referrals and the keeping of registers.

The Civic Agenda Authority validates the plurality of the pools and of the training programmes. Any provision of expertise by the General Authority may be challenged before the Civic Agenda Authority, under the conditions of Article [31](#apc-missions).

### Article 28 — Prohibitions {#agcc-interdictions}

The task of the General Authority for Citizens' Conventions is exclusively one of constitutional stewardship. It organises deliberation materially without intervening in its content, its political orientations, its scientific controversies or its constitutional review, which fall to the competent authorities.

The office of Director-General is incompatible with any ministerial office, any external representation office and any office in another constitutional authority.

The organic law shall lay down the mechanisms preventing the capture, corruption, covert influence or bureaucratisation of this authority.

## Chapter II — The Civic Agenda Authority

### Article 29 — Nature and composition {#apc-nature-composition}

The Civic Agenda Authority is an independent constitutional authority composed of fifty citizens drawn by lot according to the representativeness criteria defined in Article [12](#composition-representativite).

Members serve a one-year term, which is not immediately renewable. Half of the membership is renewed every six months in order to ensure continuity of work.

Members enjoy the status of convention members defined in Article [14](#statut-conventionnel). By way of derogation from Article [14](#statut-conventionnel), the identity of its members remains confidential during their term of office and is made public only at its end; its deliberations are published in a form that does not allow its members to be identified.

### Article 30 — Functioning {#apc-fonctionnement}

The Civic Agenda Authority sits every month. It may be convened in extraordinary session when circumstances so require, in particular in order to:

- consider whether to open a thematic convention following a petition reaching the threshold laid down in Article [33](#seuils-effets-petitions);

- refer a matter to the Constitutional Council in the cases provided for by this Constitution;

- convene an additional session of the annual ordinary convention in the cases provided for by this Constitution.

The organic law shall lay down the procedures for convening an extraordinary session and the applicable quorum and majority rules.

### Article 31 — Tasks {#apc-missions}

The Civic Agenda Authority:

- publicly lists referrals from citizens' petitions, citizens' conventions, executive administrations, local authorities, constitutional authorities, oversight authorities, courts and the Defender of Rights;

- forwards all referrals to the agenda-setting convention;

- implements the programme defined by the agenda-setting convention by opening thematic conventions according to the planned timetable;

- ensures the institutional continuity of legislative activity between two agenda-setting conventions;

- proposes to each citizens' convention the relevant sub-specialities of the expertise pools for the subject under consideration;

- validates the effective plurality of the expertise pools drawn upon for each convention and for each appointment to judicial offices filled by sortition, as well as the training programmes for convention members and their trainers. It may require the modification of a pool or of a training programme for lack of plurality.

The plurality of the pools is assessed according to objective, verifiable indicators laid down by the organic law, including in particular:

- the diversity of initial training institutions;

- geographical distribution;

- the diversity of sectors of activity;

- the diversity of doctrinal or professional positions, assessed on the basis of publications, offices held and identifiable contributions in the speciality.

Referrals from the courts take the form of collective public reports, adopted by the court sitting in plenary formation. They may not concern a case pending before the referring court. The procedures for these referrals are specified by the organic law.

Any convention member, any member of an expertise pool and any interested person may challenge before the Civic Agenda Authority the plurality of a pool, the choice of sub-specialities drawn upon, the expertise provided, the training programme or the validated trainers. The Authority shall rule within one month.

### Article 32 — Prohibitions and openings outside the programme {#apc-interdictions}

The Civic Agenda Authority does not vote on the law and does not decide the content of the legislative programme, which falls to the agenda-setting convention.

It opens thematic conventions in application of the programme set by the agenda-setting convention. It may open them outside the programme, by reasoned and public decision, only in the following cases:

- on petition, under the conditions laid down in Article [33](#seuils-effets-petitions);
- at the request of the plenary session of the Law Implementation Oversight Authority;
- at the request of the agenda-setting convention or of the annual ordinary convention, in particular for a major tax reform;
- on a reasoned proposal from the High Council of the Judiciary, concerning the status of members of the judiciary or the organisation of the judiciary;
- for a draft act of the European Union or for the transposition of such an act;
- for the authorisation of a military operation, under the conditions laid down in Article [61](#autorisation-operations-militaires);
- where a definite legal deadline so requires;

- where this Constitution provides for a dedicated thematic convention.

Excluding petitions, military authorisations, thematic conventions provided for by this Constitution, transpositions of an act of the European Union and definite legal deadlines, the number of thematic conventions opened outside the programme may not exceed each year a ceiling laid down by the organic law. Any decision to open or to refuse may be appealed before the Constitutional Council, under the conditions laid down by the organic law.

Crisis situations fall to the emergency convention, under the conditions laid down in Title [XII](#crises).

### Article 33 — Thresholds and effects of citizens' petitions {#seuils-effets-petitions}

Citizens' petitions concerning the legislative agenda are received under two distinct regimes:

- any petition reaching a threshold laid down by the organic law is publicly listed by the Civic Agenda Authority and forwarded to the next agenda-setting convention, which examines it in the course of its work;

- any petition reaching a threshold of signatures equal to one per cent of the civic body obliges the Civic Agenda Authority to consider, within a time limit laid down by the organic law, whether to open a dedicated thematic convention on the subject of the petition.

The consideration provided for in the second paragraph gives rise to a reasoned and public decision. The Civic Agenda Authority may decide either to open a thematic convention, or to forward the petition to the next agenda-setting convention, or, by reasoned decision, to take no further action. Its decision may be appealed before the Constitutional Council under the conditions laid down by the organic law.

The other petition thresholds, in particular those laid down in Articles [156](#referendum-transferts-intermediaires) and [163](#revision-initiative) for European referendums and constitutional amendments, remain governed by the specific provisions of this Constitution.

### Article 34 — Safeguards against capture {#apc-garanties-contre-capture}

Membership of the Civic Agenda Authority is incompatible with any executive, constitutional review or party leadership office.

The organic law shall lay down the mechanisms preventing the capture, corruption, covert influence or bureaucratisation of this authority.
