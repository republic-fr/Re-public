# Preamble

National sovereignty belongs to the people, who exercise it through popular deliberation that is informed, pluralist and protected against domination, haste, manipulation and the concentration of power.

Civic sortition is the normal means of appointing the bodies called upon to deliberate in the name of the people and to form the general will.

The law is legitimate only if it results from a procedure of collective deliberation ensuring the representativeness of all components of the people, and respects this Constitution, fundamental rights and the best-established scientific knowledge.

The implementation of the law is subordinate to the law and continuously overseen by citizens, by judges and by guarantor authorities.

The Republic ensures the equal dignity of all persons and protects them against the tyranny of the majority, discrimination and arbitrariness.

This Constitution establishes a deliberative democratic Republic.
