<p class="lead">This text is a draft. It is meant to be discussed, corrected and improved, article by article.</p>

## <span class="kicker">01</span>Comment on an article

Under each article, the **Comment** button opens that article's discussion thread. You can ask a question there, point out an inconsistency or argue for different wording.

Comments are hosted on GitHub Discussions: you need a free GitHub account to post. Anyone can read them.

<p><a class="rp-btn rp-btn--primary" href="/en/text/">Read the text and comment</a></p>

## <span class="kicker">02</span>Propose a change

The text is kept on GitHub, one file per title. To propose new wording:

1. open the file for the relevant title in the [project repository](https://github.com/{{repo}}/tree/main/constitution);
2. click the pencil icon to edit it, directly in your browser;
3. describe your proposal and submit it: it becomes a "pull request", which the team and other contributors can review and discuss, then merge or not.

Every change that is merged remains visible in the text's history.

## <span class="kicker">03</span>Start a substantive debate

For a question that goes beyond a single article (a choice of principle, how several titles fit together), open a [discussion](https://github.com/{{repo}}/discussions) or an [issue](https://github.com/{{repo}}/issues).

## <span class="kicker">04</span>Discussion rules

- Discuss the text, not the people.
- One idea per comment, with the article concerned.
- Propose wording when you criticise a formulation.
- Abusive, discriminatory, promotional or off-topic messages are removed.

No GitHub account? [Write to us](/en/contact/).
